## Walmart — Walmart.com Affiliate Program

- **Network:** Impact (Impact Radius) — Walmart's own FAQ names Impact Radius
  as its Affiliate Service Provider.
- **Free to join:** Yes — Walmart's FAQ: "It is absolutely free to join the
  Walmart.com Affiliate Program."
- **Commission (public):** up to 4% on eligible sales, category-dependent;
  exact rates are inside the Impact dashboard.
- **Key terms:** commissions on monthly net sales excluding returns, tax and
  shipping; returned/refunded orders are debited back; approval is at
  Walmart's discretion and is not guaranteed by creating an Impact account.
- **Disclosure:** FTC disclosure adjacent to every affiliate link; the deals
  schema stores per-program disclosure text and renders it next to the link.
- **Registration email:** pptvinstall@gmail.com
- **Status:** NOT REGISTERED
