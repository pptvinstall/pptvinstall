# PPTVInstall Customer-Facing Marketing Rules

This file is the canonical source for customer-facing claims used by the website, social posts, ads, email copy, and future AI-generated marketing.

## Safe claims

- Picture Perfect TV Install provides professional TV mounting and related installation services.
- Standard TV mounting starts at $100 when the customer supplies the mount, unless the active pricing engine says otherwise.
- Based in Metro Atlanta and available for longer-distance jobs.
- Distance is not an automatic service-area rejection. For farther jobs, travel is priced from the route using estimated fuel/travel cost and confirmed before final booking.
- Weekday appointment availability starts after 5:30 PM.
- Weekend appointment availability is generally 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- 250+ customers is an approved proof point.
- Supported work can include drywall, brick/stone, fireplace mounting, wire concealment/outlet-behind-TV work, soundbars, selected smart-home/AV installs, dismount/remount work, and other quoted mounting projects.

## Claims that must NOT be used unless the owner explicitly updates this file

- Do not say "licensed," "licensed & insured," or "insured." Insurance is not currently confirmed for marketing use, and this type of work does not require a general licensing claim.
- Do not promise same-day service. Same-day work may happen when the schedule allows, but it is not guaranteed.
- Do not claim 24/7 availability, "call anytime," emergency service, or business hours that conflict with the active booking schedule.
- Do not claim a fixed mileage radius or reject a customer solely because they are outside Metro Atlanta.
- Do not claim 1,000+ customers, 500+ TVs, 100% satisfaction, a warranty length, referral discounts, or other numeric proof unless the current production data or owner policy specifically supports it.
- Do not claim that every smart-home, electrical, outdoor, commercial, ceiling-fan, or specialty request is automatically supported. Scope must match current operating rules.
- Do not imply PPTVInstall installs new high-voltage circuits. Electrical work is limited to the currently supported scope, including appropriate existing-outlet relocation/add-on work.

## Travel wording

Preferred wording:

> Based in Metro Atlanta, but we travel for the right job. Local travel is included where applicable; farther jobs include a route-based fuel/travel amount that is confirmed before booking.

Avoid wording such as "outside our service area," "out of range," or "maximum 50-mile radius" unless the owner later reinstates a hard limit.

## Scheduling wording

Preferred wording:

> Weekday appointments begin after 5:30 PM, with weekend availability generally from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Available times shown during booking are the source of truth.

Do not promise same-day or immediate appointments unless a specific opening has actually been confirmed.

## Pricing wording

Use "starts at" language unless the quote tool has enough information to produce a firm line item. Travel, unusual access, specialty walls, fireplace concealment, extra electrical scope, multi-stop jobs, and custom AV/smart-home work may require confirmation.

## AI-agent rule

Any AI agent editing marketing, SEO, homepage copy, social copy, email copy, or structured metadata must check this file first and must not introduce stronger claims than these rules allow.
